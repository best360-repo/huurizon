Accuracy and safety

Sun, moon, shadow and terrain results are calculated estimates. Elevation data can miss nearby features such as buildings, trees and small ridges, and weather forecasts can be wrong. Always check real conditions yourself.

Do not rely on the app for your safety. Take particular care near cliffs, water and tides, in mountains, and when walking before sunrise or after sunset.
