Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

The short version. No account, no ads, no analytics or tracking. The developer runs no servers and never receives your location or any other personal data. The app uses your location on your phone to work out the sun and moon. To show maps, search and terrain, it asks the services below for data, so they see the area you are looking at.

Who we are

Huurizon is made by an independent developer ("we"), named as the seller on the App Store listing. Contact: hello@best360.co.uk.

What we keep

Nothing. We have no database or server, so there is no personal data about you for us to store, share, sell or delete.

Your choices

  • You can use the app without sharing your location: drop a pin on the map instead.
  • You can turn location access off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Saved spots synced to iCloud can be removed in Settings → [your name] → iCloud.

Children

The app is not aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data we hold about you. We hold none, but you are welcome to ask. For data held by Apple or Google, use their privacy tools. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
